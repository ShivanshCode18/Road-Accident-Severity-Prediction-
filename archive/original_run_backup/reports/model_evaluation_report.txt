3. ACADEMIC MODEL DIAGNOSTICS & COMPARATIVE WEAKNESS ANALYSIS
================================================================================

A. The Minority Class Dilemma (Fatal Severity, 1.43% Prevalence):
   - In road traffic collision datasets, fatal crashes are extremely rare (~1.4% of total).
   - Standard, unweighted tree algorithms (Decision Tree, Random Forest) prioritize global
     accuracy (minimizing empirical classification error). Because predicting 'Slight' for
     every collision guarantees ~73.8% accuracy, unweighted algorithms exhibit low recall on
     Fatal and Serious accidents.

B. Multinomial Logistic Regression with Balanced Class Weighting:
   - By applying inverse-class-frequency sample weights, Logistic Regression is penalized
     23.3x more heavily for misclassifying a Fatal accident than a Slight accident.
   - This shifts the decision boundary, significantly raising Fatal Recall and Macro F1-score,
     making it medically and operationally actionable for emergency road safety planning.

C. Decision Tree & Random Forest Ensemble Characteristics:
   - Random Forest produces superior probability calibration and non-linear feature interaction
     capturing (e.g. speed zone * unlit lighting * road surface).
   - However, without extreme cost-sensitive splits, tree algorithms maintain higher precision
     at the expense of lower minority class recall.

4. FINAL MODEL SELECTION: LOGISTIC REGRESSION
   - Selected based on balanced Macro F1 and minority safety recall rather than raw accuracy alone.
   - The trained pipeline and model artifacts are preserved in models/.
