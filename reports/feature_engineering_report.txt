1. FEATURE SELECTION PHILOSOPHY & LEAKAGE PREVENTION PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In traffic crash injury modeling, feature engineering must be strictly aligned with
the operational state known at or prior to collision occurrence. Any post-crash
outcome (such as emergency room admissions, casualty counts, or police dispatch logs)
constitutes direct target leakage, creating artificially inflated evaluation metrics
that catastrophically fail in real-world deployment.

The following attributes are strictly excluded from predictive features:
1. 'number_of_casualties': Outcome consequence of collision severity.
2. 'enhanced_severity_collision': Alternative DfT post-hoc severity classification.
3. 'collision_injury_based': Indicator of whether injury was recorded post-hoc.
4. 'collision_adjusted_severity_serious': DfT imputed probability of serious injury.
5. 'collision_adjusted_severity_slight': DfT imputed probability of slight injury.
6. 'did_police_officer_attend_scene_of_accident': Post-accident emergency response.
7. 'collision_index', 'collision_ref_no': Unique incident identifiers.
8. 'collision_year': Constant value (2025) providing zero variance.

2. CONTINUOUS CYCLICAL TEMPORAL ENCODINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Standard linear integer representations of time (hour: 0..23, month: 1..12) introduce
an artificial mathematical discontinuity: hour 23 and hour 0 appear 23 units apart
instead of 1 unit apart; month 12 (December) and month 1 (January) appear 11 units
apart instead of 1 unit apart.

To preserve circular continuity across midnight and the New Year transition, continuous
sine and cosine transformations were implemented:

1. sin_hour = sin(2 * pi * hour / 24.0)
   Domain: [-1.0, 1.0]. Captures the cyclical diurnal rhythm.
2. cos_hour = cos(2 * pi * hour / 24.0)
   Domain: [-1.0, 1.0]. Complements sin_hour to establish a unique 2D coordinate on the
   24-hour circle.
3. sin_month = sin(2 * pi * month / 12.0)
   Domain: [-1.0, 1.0]. Captures seasonal progression (winter vs summer).
4. cos_month = cos(2 * pi * month / 12.0)
   Domain: [-1.0, 1.0]. Complements sin_month for circular calendar continuity.

3. DOMAIN-SPECIFIC HAZARD INTERACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Empirical road safety research demonstrates that accident severity is driven by
compounding physical risk factors rather than isolated variables. Five cross-domain
interaction features were engineered:

1. road_speed_inter = road_type_name + "_" + speed_zone
   Rationale: Differentiates speed effects by road geometry. For instance, high speed
   (60-70 mph) on a Single Carriageway presents extreme head-on collision danger,
   whereas 70 mph on a Dual Carriageway benefits from physical central reservation barriers.

2. weather_surface_inter = weather_group + "_" + road_surface_group
   Rationale: Captures dynamic tire-pavement friction states. Adverse weather (Raining,
   Snow/Ice) interacting with Wet or Icy pavement significantly lengthens braking distance
   and increases impact velocities.

3. light_time_inter = light_group + "_" + time_of_day
   Rationale: Unlit darkness during late night reflects combined visibility deprivation,
   driver fatigue, and potential alcohol impairment, whereas darkness with lights lit
   during evening rush hour represents congested, lower-speed urban travel.

4. weekend_time_inter = weekday_weekend + "_" + time_of_day
   Rationale: Distinguishes weekend night leisure travel (disproportionately associated
   with high-speed, severe collisions) from weekday morning and evening work commuting.

5. urban_rural_speed_inter = urban_rural_name + "_" + speed_zone
   Rationale: Rural high-speed corridors account for over 61% of all UK traffic fatalities
   due to undivided roadways, roadside obstacles, and extended emergency medical response times.
   This feature isolates rural high-speed routes from dense urban networks.

6. high_speed_single_carriageway (Binary: 1.0 / 0.0)
   Definition: (road_type_name == "Single carriageway") & (speed_limit >= 60)
   Rationale: Single carriageways with speed limits >= 60 mph represent the single
   highest fatality hazard profile identified in UK Department for Transport STATS19 bulletins.
